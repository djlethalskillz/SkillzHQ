## A. Audit Summary

The archive is a compact, high-signal folder: 63 images, 10.3 MB, zero video files. Roughly half of the assets are named photographs of DJ Lethal Skillz with internationally recognisable figures from hip-hop and turntablism (Grandmaster Flash, DJ Q-Bert, DJ Jazzy Jeff, Tony Touch, DJ Apollo, Vinroc, Rob Swift, DJ Nu-Mark, Keith Shocklee, Grand Master Sueside of Wu-Tang Clan, DJ Cut Killer, DJ Naughty J of NTM, MIMS, M1 of Dead Prez, Pharoahe Monch, Big Ali, the Scratch Perverts, the Japanese DMC champions). The other half are Facebook-exported photographs with opaque filenames whose subjects could not be identified without human review.

The editorial hypothesis holds. The archive strongly supports "SKILLZ AT THE CENTRE": the material reads as a constellation of documented encounters and shared stages, not a celebrity wall. Every named figure appears in a photograph that includes Skillz, and the filenames themselves record the relationship. The strongest surviving asset for the hero role is the Grandmaster Flash photograph; its file quality is modest (1024x1024, dark, soft), but its significance is the highest in the archive (see Section I).

Identities in this report are claimed only where the filename, folder, EXIF, or existing project evidence supports them (per the identity verification rule). Everything else is flagged for human confirmation.

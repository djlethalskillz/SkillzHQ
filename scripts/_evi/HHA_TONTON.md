INTRODUCTION
In its first season of Hip-Hoppin’ Asia,
Malaysia's biggest Hip Hop artiste, Joe Flizzow
finds the best places to see, the best people to
meet, the best things to do, and the best Hip Hop
experiences that Asians have to offer.
The much anticipated second season sees
Malaysia’s most promising hip-hop artiste,
SonaOne travel into the heart of Asia in search of
the answer to the ultimate question. Has hip-hop
been around since the dawn of civilization? Join
him as he journeys all across Asia to discover like
minded individuals and investigate the
phenomenal growth of hip-hop in the deeper
reaches of the continent.
The internationally recognized show, Hip-
Hoppin’ Asia, returns with a renewed sense of
adventure on the back of its award-winning first
season.

SYNOPSIS
Hip-Hoppin’ Asia; The Saga Continues is a
travelogue/reality program that features a hip-hop
proponent which returns with a renewed sense of
adventure on the back of its award-winning first
season.
Malaysia's freshest Hip Hop artiste,
SonaOne finds the best places to see, the best
people to meet, the best things to do, and the best
Hip Hop experiences that Asians have to offer.
The host embarks on a journey across Asia
to meet and collaborate with like-minded
individuals, and along the way, explore the streets
and music that make Hip-Hop relevant to the
respective country. Meet the people and cultures
that have embraced this western genre, and
explore the power of its influence.
